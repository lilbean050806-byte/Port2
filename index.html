The Case
CASE 07

He believes he killed her.

The Bunker is a psychological horror survival game built around uncertainty. A man comes home to find his wife dead, flees from the police, and hides underground. Players explore abandoned environments, collect evidence, avoid enemies, and follow a story that grows less and less trustworthy.

Rather than relying only on traditional horror enemies, the project uses uncertainty itself as a mechanic. The player keeps asking:

Is this real?Can I trust what I am seeing?Am I following the right path?What actually happened?

Design Challenge

Designing
uncertainty

The central challenge was making uncertainty a gameplay mechanic rather than a narrative theme. Horror works when players can't tell whether what they find is trustworthy, but playtesting showed where that turned into plain confusion.

NO. 01

Navigation

Players couldn't tell what to do next.
  • Directional arrow that flips to "return to bunker"
  • Objective and completion banners
  • Controls moved to the splash screen
  • Prompt corrected from E to match the real key
NO. 02

Readability

Clues melted into the dark.
  • Glow on collectible evidence
  • Lighter darkness overlay, wider flashlight
  • Highlights on nearby interactables
  • Items spread out, not clustered at the checkpoint
NO. 03

Hallucination

Doubt had to feel designed, not random.
  • False clues that summon the ghost wife
  • Real vs. fake doors and evidence
  • An unexplained life loss investigated to protect player trust
  • Honest counters against dishonest surroundings
The Hallucination System

What if the
player is wrong?

Uncertainty becomes part of interaction. The game separates information that reflects the true game state from cues that only look trustworthy, and the player has to decide which is which.

Level Breakdown

Three
case files

Exhibit 01 — the streets, after dark
FILE 01

The Scavenge

The first level introduces the game's core systems: top-down exploration, a flashlight cutting through the dark, collectible supplies and evidence, searchable dumpsters, police cars on patrol, and the bunker as a checkpoint.

ExploreCollectAvoidReturn
Exhibit 02 — the sewer maze
FILE 02

The Sewer

Exploration becomes maze navigation. Narrow underground corridors, a countdown, and a tunnel exit leading toward the police station. Playtesting said the first maze was too tight and too short on time, so the paths were widened, the timer extended, and Pac-Man-style variation planned.

NavigateCollectBeat the clockExit
Exhibit 03 — 6th District Precinct, night shift
FILE 03

The Police Office

The final level changes the rules. The player searches the abandoned precinct for evidence under a tight flashlight, and some of what they find is not real. Visibility fixes here kept the darkness frightening without hiding the clues.
