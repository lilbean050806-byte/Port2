Strat-Connect

An app for students, made by students. It brings Stratford’s events, restaurants and student discounts into one place for University of Waterloo GBDA students on the Stratford campus.

The problem

Students come for lectures, then leave.

The Stratford campus has no library, gym, cafeteria or student centre. Students often miss events on main campus, know little about what happens in Stratford, and get stranded between bus times with nothing to do. These can seem like small issues, but they shape the whole student experience.

Research

What students told us

I interviewed three students (two women, one man): commuters from Waterloo and one Stratford resident. Interviews took place in a relaxed project room, were anonymous, and followed a guide whose probes I adapted to each person. The notes were clustered into four themes.

Limited facilities

No library, gym, cafeteria or student centre. A wish for somewhere to relax, study or socialise.

Transport & waiting

Unreliable bus schedules leave students stranded, so they leave early or wait with little to do.

Social isolation

A quiet campus with few clubs or gatherings, and fewer chances to meet people.

Low local awareness

Little knowledge of cafés, events and student discounts. Most rarely venture beyond campus.

“Stranded”
“In their own world”
“No one really knows about them”

Short phrases from the interview report. The research is small and qualitative, so these are themes, not statistics.

High-fidelity design

The original screens

A warm maroon-and-blush identity with soft cloud shapes, a 3D location pin as the brand mark, and a gold hand-lettered wordmark. Scroll sideways.

Prototype

Try it yourself

The Figma flow rebuilt as a clickable prototype: Connect, login, home events, search categories, food and discounts, event details, reviews and your saved events.

Interactions

Top navigation, search, category lists, ratings, hearts to save, calendar toggle, review tabs.

Small fixes

Added labels for screen readers, a fixed “Restaurants” typo, and empty states.

Event dates, offers and reviews are sample content from the design. Confirm real details before relying on them.

Reflection

What I learned

Letting the interviews flow naturally surfaced the strongest insights, such as being “stuck waiting for the bus” and not knowing what to do between classes. Next steps: test with more students, replace sample content with verified listings, add a time-based “what can I do in 45 minutes?” filter, and make the top navigation labels visible.
